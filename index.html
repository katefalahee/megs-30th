      Getting it on the telly

      1. Laptop and HDMI cable

        The most reliable way. Plug the laptop into the TV, open this link in Chrome, Edge or Safari, and go full screen with F11 on Windows or Ctrl Cmd F on a Mac.

      2. Chromecast or Google TV

        Open the link in Chrome on a laptop, open the ⋮ menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab and pick the TV.

      3. Apple TV or an AirPlay TV

        Open the link on an iPhone, iPad or Mac and use Screen Mirroring to send it to the TV.

      4. Fire TV or Android TV

        Open the link in the TV's web browser (Silk on a Fire TV). Move around with the arrows on the remote and press the middle button to choose. The back button takes you back.
